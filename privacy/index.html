MIUI ADS HELPER / PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

MIUI Ads Helper is an independent app provided by Tejas Gajjar. This policy explains data use in the current app and on this public information website.

Effective date: 9 November 2026

Information the app processes

  • Device and software information: a device identifier returned by the device-information library, manufacturer, model, Android version, MIUI or HyperOS version, and app/build information. The current implementation uses the device identifier as a Firebase Analytics user ID and event property.
  • Guide and settings activity: screens viewed, guides opened, attempted settings destinations, and whether opening succeeded.
  • Relevant app availability: the app checks whether packages associated with its settings guides are present. It reports installed/missing guide IDs, counts, category summaries, and associated app labels to Firebase Analytics. This is not a scan of every app on the device.
  • Crash and diagnostic data: Firebase Crashlytics receives errors and technical diagnostics. Third-party SDKs may also process identifiers, network information, and technical data under their own policies.
  • Local preferences: appearance, language, view mode, and purchase status are stored on the device.

Why this information is used

Information is used to show relevant guides, understand compatibility and app use, diagnose failures, and improve the app. Crash reporting is enabled automatically in the current configuration. The app does not currently provide an in-app analytics opt-out switch.

Third-party services

The app uses Google services. Review their descriptions of processing and privacy practices:

  • Google Play Services / Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Analytics for Firebase
  • Firebase, including Crashlytics

Optional purchases

Where offered, purchases and purchase restoration are handled through Google Play Billing. The app processes purchase results to enable purchased features and stores purchase status locally. Payment details are handled by Google Play rather than entered into this website.

Support messages

If you contact Tejas Gajjar, information you choose to send, such as an email address, device details, or an issue description, is used to respond and investigate. GitHub issues are public; avoid sharing private information there.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It contains no analytics scripts, advertising scripts, cookie banners, or account sign-in. It does not set application cookies or use browser local storage. GitHub may process access logs, including IP addresses, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. External links are governed by the destination’s policy.

Retention and your choices

Local app preferences remain until cleared through Android app storage controls or removed with the app, subject to your device’s backup behavior. Data held by third-party providers follows the applicable service settings and policies. Clearing local app data does not automatically delete previously transmitted analytics or diagnostics.

Contact gajjartejas26@gmail.com with privacy, access, or deletion requests. Include only the information needed to identify your request. Some data may not be identifiable or retrievable by the developer.

Security

Reasonable efforts are made to protect information, but no storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Children’s privacy

The service is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact the developer so the concern can be investigated.

Changes and contact

Changes will be published on this page with an updated effective date. For questions, contact Tejas Gajjar at gajjartejas26@gmail.com.
