A few small steps to find the recommendations you want to change.

1. Find your guide

Open the app and browse All guides. Search for an app such as Music, Browser, or Security. The installed filter shows guides associated with apps detected on your device. Folder recommendations are a manual guide.

2. Read before opening

Choose a card to see its description, instructions, and special notes. Screen names and available toggles can differ by device region, MIUI or HyperOS version, and app version.

3. Open the settings

Tap the Open button if the guide has one. The app tries its known settings destinations in order. If none can open, it tries App info for an installed package. App info is a fallback: use the guide’s manual steps if the recommendation toggle is elsewhere.

4. Make the change yourself

Find the recommendation or advertising option described in the guide and change it. MIUI Ads Helper does not automatically change your device settings. Repeat the steps to reverse a choice later.

When a shortcut does not work

  • Check whether the associated app is installed.
  • Open system Settings or the target app manually and follow the guide.
  • Some manufacturer screens are unavailable to other apps even when installed.
  • For folder recommendations, open each folder and use the folder-name menu; there is no direct shortcut.

If the instructions do not match, report the issue on GitHub. Include your device model, system version, target app version, and the guide name. Do not post personal identifiers or private screenshots.
